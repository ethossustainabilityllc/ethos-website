Providing You Expertise

Bill Eger

Founder & Principal


Bill Eger, PE, CEM®, CC-P®, ISSP-SEA, CPM, LEED AP, founder of ETHOS Sustainability, has over 20 years of experience solving complex, systems-oriented climate action, sustainability leadership, and clean energy transition challenges to advance local and global progress for local and state governments, corporations, educational institutions, non-profit organizations, and industry.  


Bill has served in executive-level positions and is an experienced climate action, energy, and sustainability leader developing and executing equitable, community-wide clean energy transition & climate action plans and programs. 


Bill has served as an expert advisor on clean energy, climate action, and sustainability issues and policies to government executive leaders and policymakers.


Bill is an authority on organizational energy management, including building energy performance & energy efficiency, clean and renewable energy resources, energy resiliency, and decarbonizing and electrifying transportation systems.


Bill's expertise further includes public utility rate regulation, state net-metering and electricity reliability transparency and reporting policies, and stakeholder engagement in integrated electricity system planning processes.


Bill is skilled at developing and leveraging collaborations across a diversity of organizational stakeholders and building partnerships with private, non-profit, trade, public, and regional, State and Federal organizations.


Bill holds Computer, Electrical, and Mechanical engineering degrees, and is pursuing advanced professional development in urban planning, strategic foresight, complex systems analysis, and the use and leadership of generative and agentic artificial intelligence (AI) systems in progressive organizations. Bill has completed advanced executive and capacity-building trainings  through Harvard University's Executive Education in Sustainability Leadership program, Michigan State University's Institute for Public Utilities Advanced Regulatory Studies Program, and a range of FEMA-certified courses in Incident Command and Emergency Management.


Bill holds registrations and certifications as a Professional Engineer, a Certified Energy Manager (CEM®), an International Society of Sustainability Professionals Sustainability Associate, a certified Climate Change Professional (CC-P®), a US Green Building Council LEED Accredited Professional, an Eco-District Accredited Professional, and a Certified Public Manager. Bill is proudly an Eagle Scout and Amateur Radio Operator (KD3AUX).
